## KAST 20.2.7

- **New look.** The KAST icon and banner are now in Krinik acid pink.
- **FreeDroidWarn.** On the first launch of a new version KAST shows one dialog about Google's planned developer verification for Android apps (2026/2027) and what you can do about it ([Keep Android Open](https://keepandroidopen.org)). Tap OK and it stays quiet until the next version.
- **Install and update with [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium).** Releases are signed with the project's own key; the README button adds KAST to Obtainium with the right APK filter.

**Which APK:** most phones and tablets need `KAST-20.2.7-nonRoot-arm64-v8a.apk`; older 32-bit devices take `armeabi-v7a`, emulators and x86 tablets take `x86_64` / `x86`.

KAST installs next to Artemis and Moonlight (package `com.limelight.kast`).
