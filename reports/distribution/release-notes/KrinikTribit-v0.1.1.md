## Krinik Tribit 0.1.1 — first release

Krinik Tribit is our own native app for Tribit speakers, written from scratch in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Not affiliated with Tribit or Thousandshores; Tribit is their trademark.

- **FreeDroidWarn.** On the first launch of a new version the app shows one dialog about Google's planned developer verification for Android apps (2026/2027) and what you can do about it ([Keep Android Open](https://keepandroidopen.org)). Tap OK and it stays quiet until the next version.
- **Install and update with [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium)** — the button in the README adds Krinik Tribit in one tap.
- Released under the MIT license.
